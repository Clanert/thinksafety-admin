Delete my account

Remove your ThinkSafety account and everything stored with it.

The quickest way: from inside the app

If you still have ThinkSafety installed, this happens immediately and you do not have to wait for anybody.

  1. Open ThinkSafety and sign in.
  2. Tap Wasifu (Profile) at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Futa akaunti yangu (Delete my account).
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are removed straight away.

Or ask us here

Use this if you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in. We will find your account and remove it.

We cannot delete an account the moment this form is submitted, because a phone number typed into a web page does not prove whose account it is. We check first, so that nobody can erase somebody else's account. Requests are actioned within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, phone number or email address, and your sign-in.
  • Your training progress, quiz scores, points and rewards.
  • Your likes and comments.
  • Your orders, including the delivery address you gave.
  • Your payment records and any payment confirmation picture you uploaded.

This cannot be undone. Points and gift coupons are lost. If you sign up again later you start from the beginning.

Nothing is kept once your account is deleted.
