ThinkSafety

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

ThinkSafety is a workplace safety training app for workers in Tanzania, published by Clanert Sustain. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to get it deleted.

If you only want your account gone, go straight to Delete my account.

What we collect

Only what the app needs to work. There is no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

WhatWhy
Your nameShown on your profile and on any comment you post.
Your phone number, or your email address It is how you sign in. A phone number is also sent one verification code by SMS when you register.
Your password Stored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. Nobody at Clanert Sustain can read it.
Training progress, quiz scores, points and rewards So your course carries on where you stopped, and so points can be spent.
Likes and comments you postShown to other workers on that lesson.
Orders: items, quantity, size, delivery location and contact number So the equipment you buy can be delivered to you.
Payments: the amount, and the payment confirmation picture you attach So we can confirm a payment was made before sending goods.

What we do not collect

  • We do not read your SMS messages, your contacts, your call log or your files.
  • We do not collect your location. The delivery address is text you type yourself.
  • We do not use your camera or microphone in the background.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

The app asks for only two Android permissions: internet access, and the ability to check whether you are online. Nothing else.

Who your data reaches

  • Supabase hosts the database, the files and the sign-in system. Your data is stored there.
  • Notify Africa delivers the one verification SMS when you register with a phone number. They receive your phone number and the message, nothing else.
  • The ThinkSafety administrator receives an email when someone registers, places an order, makes a payment or posts a comment, so those can be acted on.

We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers or anybody else.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed, and that removal is immediate and permanent. Verification codes are deleted as soon as they are used, and in any case expire after ten minutes.

Your choices

  • See and correct your details. Open Profile in the app and edit your name.
  • Delete your account and everything in it. Open Profile in the app and choose Delete my account. If you no longer have the app installed, use the web form.
  • Ask a question. Email clanertsustaintz@gmail.com.

Children

ThinkSafety is workplace training and is intended for people of working age. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will remove it.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Access to the database is controlled row by row, so one worker cannot read another worker's orders, payments or progress. Passwords are hashed. Verification codes are stored only as hashes, so even a copy of our database would not reveal a code in flight.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
